Download the app (12 MB)

Open this page in Chrome on your phone.

  1. Tap Download the app. If Chrome warns the file might be harmful, tap Download anyway. The warning appears for every app that isn't from the Play Store.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open. Missed it? Open My Files → Downloads and tap SkillsTestExaminer.apk.
  3. If the phone blocks apps from this source, tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then tap back.
  4. Tap Install. If Play Protect warns, tap More details → Install anyway. If it offers a scan, tap Don't send.
  5. Open Skills Test Examiner, accept the privacy screen, and fill in your profile and signature.
  6. Tap Sign in or create an account, create your account, then type the access code Breviation gave you (e.g. STE-AB12-CD34). Each code works once and is then tied to your account – on a new phone just sign in again.
  7. Updates: come back to this page and repeat steps 1–4. Your tests stay on the phone.
